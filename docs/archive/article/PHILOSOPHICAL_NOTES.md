# Philosophical source checks and bridge assumptions

Checked 2026-11-08 for the publication rewrite; no new model inference.
These notes distinguish source positions from assumptions introduced for a proposed experiment.

## P1 — Dennett

Daniel Dennett, *Consciousness Explained* (1991), is the historical framework already cited
in `docs/SOURCES_AND_CONTEXT.md:32`. The publisher lists a later edition:
[publisher record](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/daniel-dennett/consciousness-explained/9780316180665/).
Dennett and Kinsbourne's primary paper, [Time and the Observer](https://www.contrib.andrew.cmu.edu/~kk3n/80-300/dennett-time.htm)
(1992), abstract and sections 1.1–1.2, describes distributed, revisable contents without a
single privileged presentation to an inner observer. This motivates treating reports as
constructed outputs; it does not imply that every report is temporally late or causally inert.

## P2 — Frankish

Keith Frankish, *Illusionism as a Theory of Consciousness* (2016), targets introspective
misrepresentation of phenomenal properties; see the author's [account and primary-paper links](https://www.keithfrankish.com/illusionism/).
His [edited-volume description](https://www.keithfrankish.com/illusionism-as-a-theory-of-consciousness/)
confirms that focus. Neither an action-selection error nor a numeric confidence report is
itself a measure of phenomenal properties. A software report can be misleading without
settling the philosophical theory. “Retrospective smoothing” is our operational analogy,
not a verbatim definition or a universal temporal prediction attributed to Frankish.

## P3 — Global workspace

Dehaene and Changeux, [Experimental and Theoretical Approaches to Conscious Processing](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2011.03.018)
(2011), abstract, describes global availability through a neuronal workspace; the
[primary abstract](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0896627311002583)
was checked. Our command scores do not operationalize that broadcast architecture.
Compatibility with these behavioral data is therefore not a discriminating prediction
or evidence for global workspace theory, nor evidence against it.

## P4 — Higher-order thought

David Rosenthal, [Higher-order awareness, misrepresentation and function](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22492758/)
(2012), DOI 10.1098/rstb.2011.0353, explicitly allows misrepresentation in both metacognition
and higher-order awareness and distinguishes their functions; [author-hosted paper](https://www.davidrosenthal.org/DR-Mcogn-Mrepn.pdf).
Consequently, “HOT requires reported quality to follow first-order accuracy” is not a valid
unqualified attribution. An externally displayed confidence digit is also not automatically
a consciousness-making higher-order thought. Generic HOT and the proposed manipulation
have no unique mapping without additional assumptions.

## P5 — The one operational contrast, not a theory tournament

Our report-mediated account adds three assumptions: a later self-report can differ from
task-quality evidence; the decision policy reads its recorded content; and that content
can be manipulated separately from answer competence. Under those assumptions, corrupting
the recorded category should change abstention while newly measured forced-answer accuracy
remains equivalent within a prespecified tolerance. Mere possible dissociation does not
logically entail an intervention effect: the policy-use assumption is necessary.

The restricted quality-tracking alternative says the policy follows task evidence and
ignores a counterfeit report once that evidence is fixed. This is an extra operational
model that a HOT advocate might adopt, not HOT itself. A positive or negative result
separates these restricted accounts only if manipulation and control checks pass.

At this behavioral resolution illusionism, GWT and HOT can accommodate the present data;
none supplies their numerical pattern without auxiliary implementation assumptions.
The proposed test cannot settle human experience, consciousness in Qwen, or the general
truth of any of these theories. Preserve `docs/SOURCES_AND_CONTEXT.md:30-36` as the boundary.
